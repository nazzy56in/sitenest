A few common questions

Good to know

Do I own the website?

SiteNest provides a hosted website rental service. The agreement will explain what happens to the website, content and domain if the subscription ends. Your business content remains yours.

Is a domain included?

Domain registration and renewal are quoted separately unless your written plan says otherwise. You can own your domain and point it to the hosted website.

Can I request changes?

Small content updates are included according to your plan. New pages, advanced features and major redesigns may cost extra.

Can you build an online store or booking system?

Those features require additional setup and may involve third-party services and fees. Ask us for a separate quote.
